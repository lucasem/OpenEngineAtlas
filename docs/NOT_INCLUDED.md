# Projects Not Included in the Main FOSS List

This document records notable projects that are useful to game developers but do not cleanly meet this repository's main inclusion rule.

## Defold

- Website: https://defold.com/
- Source: https://github.com/defold/defold
- License: https://defold.com/license/
- Reason: Defold is free to use and publishes its source, but the project itself describes the engine as **source available**. Its license includes a restriction against selling or commercializing the work or derivative works as a competing game-engine product. That is different from conventional OSI-style open-source licensing.

## RPG Paper Maker

- Website: https://rpg-paper-maker.com/
- Source: https://github.com/RPG-Paper-Maker/RPG-Paper-Maker
- Reason: The project publishes source, but its public project description advertises the engine as free for non-commercial use and separates free/commercial usage. Until its licensing is unambiguously FOSS for the complete engine/toolchain, it stays outside the main list.

## Proprietary engines with free tiers

Unity, Unreal Engine, GameMaker, Construct and similar proprietary engines are outside scope even when they can be used without an upfront license fee.

## Why keep this file?

A curated list is more useful when it explains boundary cases. “Source visible,” “free to download,” and “open source” are not interchangeable terms.
